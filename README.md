# Liquicity

Liquicity is a global payments platform built to make cross-border transfers feel instant. The goal is to replace the slow experience of SWIFT and traditional remittance rails with a solution that settles transactions in seconds instead of days.

## Overview

Liquicity lets users link their bank, complete KYC, and start moving money right away—no need for a crypto wallet or prior knowledge of stablecoins.

* **Instant Settlement:** Converts funds into stablecoins behind the scenes, locks in the real mid-market FX rate, and settles transfers on chain, eliminating delays and hidden fees from intermediary banks.
* **Seamless Off-Ramp:** Recipients can receive USD via a virtual account or instantly off-ramp into their local currency through connected banking partners.
* **Ideal for Freelancers, Small Businesses & Merchants:** Designed for predictable fees and rapid settlement, outperforming traditional fintech apps.

## Key Features

- **Global Transfers**: Send money internationally in seconds.
- **Virtual US Bank Accounts**: For non-US users to receive USD without needing a US bank.
- **Visa Card**: Spend in 170+ countries.
- **Real-Time Reporting**: Track every send, receive, deposit, and withdrawal with instant analytics.
- **Business Dashboards**: Manage payments, invoicing, batch payouts, and cash flow across regions.
- **API Suite**: Embed global payments, automate payouts, and access liquidity tools from your own platform.

## How It Works

1. **Link your bank account**
2. **Complete KYC (Know Your Customer)**
3. **Start moving money** — instantly and globally.
4. **Receive payments** via virtual accounts or off-ramp into local currencies.

All FX conversions use real mid-market rates. Transfers are settled on-chain for speed, transparency, and cost efficiency.
